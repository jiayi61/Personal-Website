Trading firms run some of the best strategy games I have played, and most people never get to try them. I am rebuilding my favorites here, one at a time.

    Blotto

    You have 100 soldiers and 10 castles. Castle k is worth k points, and whoever sends more soldiers takes it. There is no single best answer. The best split depends on what everyone else does, so the real game is guessing them.
